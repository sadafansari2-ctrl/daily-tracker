📋 Rules
🥗 Diet Rules▼
  • Max 1,800 kcal/day
  • 160-170g protein daily
  • Chicken keema + 1 roti or 1-2 dosas at lunch
  • No carbs at dinner
  • 3+ litres water daily
💪 Workout Rules▼
  • Gym 6 days/week, morning preferred
  • 45-60 min strength training
  • 8,000-10,000 steps daily
  • No Mastar
🧠 Discipline▼
  • Sleep 7+ hours
  • 1 controlled meal every 10-14 days
  • Track calories daily
  • Weigh once per week
📵 No Phone 9PM-9AM▼
  • No social media 9pm-9am
  • Phone away 30 min before sleep
  • Better sleep = better fat loss
⚠️ Avoid▼
  • Extra oil & bites
  • Liquid calories
  • Skipping protein
  • Weekend overeating
💡 Trainer Tips
🥗 Nutrition
  • Eat protein first every meal
  • Pre-workout: banana + black coffee
  • Post-workout: whey/eggs within 30 min
  • Dinner: paneer/eggs/chicken + salad
  • Chew slowly — fullness takes 20 min
  • Avoid packaged "diet" foods
💪 Training
  • Progressive overload every 2 weeks
  • Compound first: squat, deadlift, bench
  • Rest 60–90 sec between sets
  • NEAT matters — stairs, walk after meals
  • No Mastar = discipline mindset
  • Track lifts to measure improvement
😴 Recovery
  • Sleep = peak fat loss hormones (GH)
  • Poor sleep → cortisol → fat storage
  • Cold rinse post-workout reduces DOMS
  • Stretch 5 min after every session
  • Deload week every 6 weeks
📈 Track Progress
  • Weigh Sunday morning (post-toilet)
  • Progress pics every 2 weeks
  • Measure waist, chest, hips monthly
  • Track energy not just the scale
  • Plateau? Drop 100 kcal or +10 min walk
  • A bad week ≠ failure. Just resume.
